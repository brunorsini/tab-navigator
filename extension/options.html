Keyboard shortcuts

The cycle shortcut opens a visual recent-tab list. Repeat it to move the highlight; reverse moves back. Your current page stays active until you press Enter. Escape or clicking outside cancels. The list stays open when you release the modifiers. The last-tab shortcut still toggles immediately between two tabs.

Keyboard Maestro: Command + Option + Tab sends Control + Shift + 7; adding Shift sends Control + Shift + 8. Your existing mappings work as they are. No release macro or extra confirmation shortcut is needed.

Fast by design. Private by default.
No accounts, analytics, AI downloads, or remote search requests. Everything is searched on your device. Incognito is excluded. Page-text search skips form fields and editable areas; other page text may contain personal information.
