Keyboard shortcuts

Some shortcuts are unassigned. Use Customize in Chrome to choose keys.

These are your current shortcuts. The defaults use Q for a quick return and R for recent tabs; add Shift to reverse. Repeat the cycle shortcut or use ↑/↓, then press Enter to switch. Escape or clicking outside cancels. Releasing the keys leaves the list open. Jump to last tab switches immediately; press it again to return.

Prefer the mouse? Pin Tab Navigator in Chrome’s Extensions menu, then click the spyglass to search. You can change any shortcut above if it conflicts with another app or your keyboard layout.

Fast by design. Private by default.
No accounts, analytics, AI downloads, or remote search requests. Everything is searched on your device. Incognito is excluded. Page-text search skips form fields and editable areas; other page text may contain personal information.
